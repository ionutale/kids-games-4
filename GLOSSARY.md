# Cozy Nook

Cozy Nook is a mobile-first web app (installable PWA) of calm learning games for kids aged 5–8. All progress lives on the device; there are no accounts and no network calls at runtime.

## Language

**Game**:
A self-contained playable activity with its own tile on the home scene, its own ten levels, and its own learning goal.
_Avoid_: mode, mini-game, activity

**Level**:
One of the ten difficulty steps inside a game. Level N+1 unlocks only after every round of level N is finished.
_Avoid_: stage, chapter, step

**Round**:
A single task inside a level — one question the kid answers before moving on. Finishing all rounds completes the level.
_Avoid_: question, turn, task

**Unlock**:
Making the next level playable once the current level is completed. Unlocking is permanent per profile.
_Avoid_: open, release

**Hint**:
Staged help that appears when a kid is stuck: a gentle nudge, then a strong hint, then a reveal. Hints only help; they never punish.
_Avoid_: clue, tip, cheat

**Celebration**:
The feedback after a win — a small sparkle-and-chime cheer for every correct answer, a big confetti-and-music moment when a level is completed.
_Avoid_: reward, prize

**Profile**:
A kid on this device: a name and an animal avatar. Progress and the active selection attach to a profile.
_Avoid_: account, user, player

**Avatar**:
The animal picture a kid picks for their profile.
_Avoid_: icon, character

**Parent Gate**:
The three-second press that reveals grown-up settings, protecting kids from accidental changes.
_Avoid_: lock screen, PIN

**Mascot**:
Bufi, the friendly owl who hosts the home scene and delivers hints, cheers, and gentle guidance.
_Avoid_: buddy, character (when Bufi is meant)

**Home**:
The cozy scene that hosts the game tiles, Bufi, and the active profile.
_Avoid_: menu, dashboard, lobby
